Scaling a recipe
================

Scale factor = desired servings / original servings. Multiply every mass or
volume by that factor. Salt, leaveners and strong spices do not scale
linearly: use 0.8x the factor above 2x, and taste at the halfway point.

Example: 4 servings to 10 gives a factor of 2.5. 320 g flour becomes 800 g;
6 g salt becomes 12 g, not 15 g.
